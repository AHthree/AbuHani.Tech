Five Ways to Improve the E-commerce Customer Experience

Practical improvements for product pages, checkout, delivery information and customer support that can reduce friction in an online store.

ABUHANI.TECH Editorial·2026-10-25·6 min read

Customers judge an online store across the entire journey, not just the product image or price. Clear information and reliable expectations help shoppers decide whether to buy and what to expect after checkout.

Make product information specific

Use accurate titles, useful images, dimensions, materials, compatibility details and care instructions where relevant. Be clear about what is included in the package and avoid claims that cannot be verified.

Explain delivery and returns clearly

Show realistic delivery estimates, applicable fees and return conditions before customers reach the final step. Policies should match the store’s actual operations and local requirements.

Reduce checkout friction

Request only information needed to fulfil the order. Make payment and delivery steps easy to understand, and test checkout on common mobile screen sizes.

Offer helpful support

Give shoppers a clear way to ask product questions before purchasing and to get help after ordering. Set realistic response expectations and use consistent answers for recurring questions.

Learn from customer signals

Review product questions, support contacts, abandoned checkouts, returns and feedback. Repeated confusion can indicate a product-page or process issue that should be fixed at its source.

Putting it into practice

A better e-commerce experience combines accurate product information, transparent policies, simple checkout and dependable support. Improve the points where customers hesitate or need help.
